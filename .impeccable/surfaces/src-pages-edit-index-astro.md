# Surface brief: /edit/ (concept one)

Scope: the whole home page at `src/pages/edit/index.astro`. Visitor mode: Persuade.

Audience and job: a Singapore buyer, mostly on a phone, arriving from Carousell or search with a budget and no confidence about parts. Action: start a WhatsApp chat, ideally about a specific build. Proof: 5.0 from 1,682 Carousell reviews, verbatim buyer quotes, listed prices as of 1 Oct 2026, the shop's own build photos.

Constraints: static Astro on GitHub Pages; no cart; imagery is the client's own Carousell listing photos, cropped to remove overlaid spec text; no live builder.

Direction was pinned by the user ("this style", the ÉTOFFE retail demo the client named), so the concept roll was not used to choose it.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A fashion-storefront treatment of PCs: the builds are shown like a season's collection, and the shop's refusal to upsell is the headline claim. It refuses the category default of dark gamer chrome and discount banners.

OWN-WORLD: Cool porcelain ground with ink-navy text, so the RGB photos are the colour. Bodoni Moda for display with an italic second line in slate; Hanken Grotesk for text and small tracked caps. Pill buttons with a round arrow badge, rectangular block button, hairline rules, cut-out PCs on near-white tiles.

STORY: These are beautiful machines; they are built to my budget; this shop does not upsell; here is who they are; here are builds with prices; other buyers back it up; message them.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A ring of twelve build photos curves across the top two thirds like the inside of a cylinder, drifting and draggable. A cut-out black RGB PC stands centre front, overlapping the ring. Bottom left: h1 "Built to / your budget." with the WhatsApp pill beneath. Bottom right: a short right-aligned paragraph and an italic signature. Header: wordmark, two facts, stacked nav, pill.

FORM: Editorial storefront after the ÉTOFFE reference (user-pinned; seed key 20e1470d was rolled but not applied). Signature interaction: the draggable photo ring. Motion grammar: ring drift, a marquee, hover reveals on products, two photos drifting at different rates in the shop section.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.

Unresolved: real logo files; client approval of photos and quotes; professional photography to replace listing images.
