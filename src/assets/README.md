# Image provenance

Every image in `builds/` is a product photo from Excelsior Systems' own public
Carousell listings (https://www.carousell.sg/u/excelsior.systems/), downloaded
on 1 October 2026 and cropped to remove the spec-sheet text that was overlaid
on the originals. They are the client's marketing images, used here for a
concept site made for that client.

`cut/` holds the same photos with the background removed (rembg, isnet model)
so the PC can sit on the page's own ground.

Before launch the client should confirm they hold the rights to each photo, or
replace it with a new shot. Nothing here was AI-generated by us.
