## Platform

web

## Stack

Astro, built as a static site and deployed to GitHub Pages (user's answer).

## Users

Singapore consumers buying a desktop PC with a budget and a use in mind (mostly gaming, some workstation and home office). Many are first-time buyers who are unsure which parts they need and wary of being oversold; others are repeat customers on a second or third rig, and some need a machine the same day. They arrive from Carousell, search, or word of mouth, mostly on a phone.

The site is being designed by the user's team for Excelsior Systems, who are the user's customer.

## Product Purpose

A marketing site for Excelsior Systems, a shop-based custom PC builder in Ang Mo Kio, Singapore. It exists to give a business that today lives only on Carousell, WhatsApp, and social pages an owned, premium presence. Success is a visitor who understands what Excelsior builds, trusts them, and starts a WhatsApp conversation with a budget or a build in hand.

## Positioning

A small shop with a seven-year, 5.0-rated track record built on honest advice: buyers repeatedly say the team does not upsell, replies fast, and builds and delivers the same day. Larger local competitors lead with scale and discounts; Excelsior leads with the person who builds the machine.

## Operating Context

- Sales happen by conversation. Buyers message on WhatsApp or Carousell, get a spec recommended to their budget, then collect from the shop or have the PC delivered.
- Builds are assembled and stress tested in the shop from brand-new parts sourced from local distributors.
- Prices and available parts change often (new GPU and CPU generations, monthly deals).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Custom gaming and workstation PCs from about S$698 to S$5,988 (Carousell listings, 1 October 2026), plus a home-office set and a few accessories.
- Services stated publicly: spec advice, assembly, stress testing, same-day collection or delivery, local warranty, Atome instalments.
- The primary action is a WhatsApp enquiry (+65 8555 0225). There is no cart or checkout.
- The site must work as static files on GitHub Pages: no server, no database.
- Undecided: the domain, who maintains prices, exact warranty terms, opening hours, and which builds to feature.

## Product Principles

1. Advice before parts. The site should make it easy to say "here is my budget, tell me what to build", not force a spec decision.
2. Proof is already earned; show it plainly. Real numbers and real words from buyers, never invented ones.
3. One build, shown well, beats a wall of listings.
4. The conversation is the conversion. Everything ends in a WhatsApp message that carries what the visitor chose.
5. Phones first. Most buyers arrive from Carousell on mobile.
